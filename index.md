# HPC Security Orientation

## Welcome

This mandatory workshop covers essential security practices and policies for using Sagehen HPC cluster. Completion is required within 30 days of account activation.

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: callout

## Pomona College HPC Context

This workshop is part of the **Pomona College Research Computing Workshop Series**
for researchers using the **Sagehen HPC cluster**.

**Prerequisites:**
- Active Sagehen HPC account (mandatory training required by ITS Policy 24 and NIST SP 800-171)
- This is mandatory training for all HPC users
- Access to the [OnDemand portal](https://ondemand.hpc.pomona.edu)

For questions or support, contact the Research Computing team at
[its-hpc@pomona.edu](mailto:its-hpc@pomona.edu).

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::

## Requirement

All new HPC users at Pomona College must complete this training within 30 days. Failure to complete will result in account suspension.

## What You'll Learn

In this workshop, you will understand:

- Your responsibilities as an HPC user
- Pomona's Acceptable Use Policy for computing resources
- Authentication methods and access controls
- Data classification and handling requirements
- How to recognize and report security incidents
- Where to get help with security questions
- How to recognize and respond to social engineering and phishing

## Workshop Structure

1. **Welcome and Responsibilities** - Your role in HPC security
2. **Acceptable Use Policy** - What you can and cannot do
3. **Authentication and Access** - Staying secure online
4. **Data Handling Basics** - Protecting research data
5. **Incident Reporting** - What to do if something goes wrong
6. **Getting Help** - Resources and contacts
7. **Social Engineering and Phishing** - Recognize and resist human-targeted attacks (NIST SP 800-171 controls 3.2.2 / 3.2.3)

Total time: Approximately 2 hours

## Success Criteria

To complete this workshop, you must:
- Complete all 7 episodes
- Understand data classification levels
- Know how to report security issues
- Acknowledge Acceptable Use Policy
- Be able to recognize phishing and social engineering attempts

## Critical Information

- **Account suspension deadline**: 30 days from account creation
- **Mandatory for**: All new HPC users
- **No exceptions**: Account will be suspended without completion
- **Compliance required**: Both policy understanding and signature
