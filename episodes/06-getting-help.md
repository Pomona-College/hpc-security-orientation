## Support Contacts

### HPC Security Issues

**Email**: its-hpc@pomona.edu

**Response time**: Within 24 hours (hours during business days)

**What to contact for**:

- Security incidents or breaches
- Policy questions or violations
- Account access problems
- Data handling advice
- Incident reporting

### ITS Help Desk

**Phone**: Check Pomona directory

**Website**: See ITS portal on Pomona website

**Response time**: Varies by ticket type

**What to contact for**:

- Authentication/login problems
- DUO setup or troubleshooting
- Password reset
- General IT issues
- Malware/security concerns on personal device

### Your PI or Advisor

**Direct**: Contact them directly

**What to discuss**:

- Data classification decisions
- Whether work is appropriate use
- Publication policies
- Data retention decisions
- Collaboration with external partners

### Compliance Office

**Via**: Your department

**What to discuss**:

- Export control questions
- FERPA data handling
- Patent and IP questions
- Confidentiality agreements

## Getting Clarification on This Training

### If Something Isn't Clear

1. **Re-read** the relevant episode
2. **Review** the learner reference guide
3. **Email its-hpc@pomona.edu** with specific question
4. **Ask in person** at office hours or by appointment

### Questions You Can Ask

- "What data classification should I use?"
- "Is this activity allowed under the policy?"
- "How do I report a security concern?"
- "Do I need to encrypt my data?"
- "Is this appropriate use?"
- Any other security or policy question

### No Question is Dumb

Honest questions show good security practice. Pretending to understand when you don't is the actual problem.

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: callout
## Default to Asking

If you're 90% sure about a security decision and 10% uncertain, ask its-hpc@pomona.edu. A one-minute email can prevent weeks of account suspension or worse. Security professionals would rather answer 100 obvious questions than have one serious incident slip through.

## Challenge: Create Your Security Action Plan

Based on what you've learned, create a personal security action plan:

1. **My account security**: How will I protect my password and DUO?
2. **My data**: What classification applies to my research? Where will I store it?
3. **My responsibilities**: What policies apply to my work?
4. **My resources**: Who will I contact if I have questions or incidents?

Write 2-3 sentences for each.

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: solution

Example answers:

1. **Account security**: "I will use a password manager to create and store a strong unique password. I will keep my DUO app updated and approved logins I didn't trigger."
2. **Data**: "My data is unpublished research, so it's PROPRIETARY classification. I will store it in /bigdata with my group. If I work with student ID numbers, I will encrypt with gocryptfs (RESTRICTED)."
3. **Responsibilities**: "I will only use Sagehen for my approved research work. I won't share my account. I will follow the Acceptable Use Policy."
4. **Resources**: "My PI for research questions, its-hpc@pomona.edu for security questions, and ITS Help Desk for account issues."
