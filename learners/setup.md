## Important Documents to Review

Before the training, please review these key policies:

### 1. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

The **Acceptable Use Policy** governs how Sagehen can be used. Summary:
- HPC resources are for approved research and educational use only
- Your PI or department must authorize your research
- You are responsible for accounts in your name
- Prohibited: illegal activity, harassment, resource waste, sharing accounts
- Violations can result in account suspension or legal action

**Location**: Check your email for AUP link, or ask its-hpc@pomona.edu

### 2. Data Classification Policy

Pomona College classifies data into **three tiers**:

**PUBLIC**: Already published, freely shareable
- Examples: Published papers, open datasets
- Storage: Anywhere
- Encryption: Not required

**PROPRIETARY**: Confidential, restricted to authorized individuals
- Examples: Draft papers, lab notes, unpublished research, novel algorithms, pre-publication data
- Storage: Sagehen
- Encryption: Recommended (gocryptfs)
- Sharing: Only with authorized people or under NDA

**RESTRICTED**: Legally protected information
- Examples: FERPA (student records), HIPAA (health data), genetic data with IDs
- Storage: Sagehen only
- Encryption: REQUIRED (gocryptfs or VeraCrypt)
- Sharing: Only with Data Use Agreement

You'll learn to classify your own data during training.

### 3. Your Institution's Regulations

Familiarize yourself with:
- **FERPA**: If you work with student records
- **HIPAA**: If you work with health/medical data
- **Export Control**: If your research involves certain technologies
- **IRB**: If your research involves human subjects

## Required Training Topics

This training covers:

1. **Why Security Matters** (15 min)
   - Real data breach scenarios
   - Costs and consequences
   - Everyone's responsibility

2. **Acceptable Use Policy** (20 min)
   - What you can/cannot do with Sagehen
   - Account sharing (prohibited)
   - Appropriate vs. inappropriate use

3. **Passwords & Authentication** (20 min)
   - Creating strong passwords
   - Using DUO Multi-Factor Authentication
   - What to do if credentials are compromised

4. **Data Classification & Protection** (20 min)
   - Three data tiers explained
   - Storage requirements per tier
   - Encryption basics
   - Access control and permissions

5. **Incident Reporting** (15 min)
   - How to recognize security incidents
   - How to report them (no-blame policy)
   - Who to contact and what information to provide

6. **Resources & Summary** (10 min)
   - Key contacts and where to get help
   - What you must acknowledge before using Sagehen

## How This Training Works

- This is **self-paced**: Work at your own speed
- **Episodes build on each other**: Complete in order
- **Hands-on exercises**: You'll complete practical scenarios
- **Key takeaways**: Marked clearly for review
- **Mandatory acknowledgment**: At the end, you'll confirm understanding

## Time Management

Plan for:
- **Total time**: 1.5-2 hours
- **Per episode**: 15-20 minutes each
- **Breaks**: Take breaks if needed; you can pause and resume
- **Note-taking**: Budget extra time if you take detailed notes

## Making the Most of This Training

1. **Read carefully**: Policies matter; don't skim
2. **Take notes**: Write down contacts, key rules, classification examples
3. **Ask questions**: During workshop or email afterward
4. **Think about your data**: How will you classify the data you work with?
5. **Plan ahead**: Before you need encryption, understand how it works

This training protects you, your research participants, Pomona College, and the broader research community. Thank you for taking it seriously.
